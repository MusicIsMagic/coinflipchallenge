Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Coin Flip Challenge runs entirely on your device. We don't have accounts, a server that receives your data, advertising, or analytics, and we don't sell or share anything, because we never have it.

What stays on your device

  • Names, nicknames, battle cries and bracket results you enter.
  • Optional contestant photos. They are resized and stored locally in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded.
  • Your settings, such as award titles.

Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes all of it.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a contestant photo, the app asks to use your camera or photo library only for that picture. Nothing else is accessed.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs. The website loads no external scripts, fonts or trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) when you visit the website.

Children

The game is made for families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions? Email us.
