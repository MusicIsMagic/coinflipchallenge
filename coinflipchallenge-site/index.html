Questions

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no accounts and no purchases.

Is the coin flip fair?

Yes. The on-screen coin uses your device's cryptographic random number generator, so it's a true 50/50 every time.

Does it need internet?

Not after the first load. The game works offline, which is handy in a backyard or a basement.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Contestant photos are shrunk and kept on your own device. We never see or upload them.

How many people can play?

Two or more. Byes are handled automatically when the bracket isn't a neat power of two.

Can I flip a real coin?

Yes. Choose "Flip a real coin", flip it on the table, then tap the side that landed.
